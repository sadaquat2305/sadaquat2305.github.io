Privacy Policy – Arrow Tangle

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Arrow Tangle ("the app") is a free, ad-supported puzzle game made by Sadaquat Ali ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, coins, themes and settings) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Depending on your choices, ads may be personalised or non-personalised. Learn how Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices and consent

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland or a U.S. state with a privacy law, the app shows a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings → Privacy settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data security and deletion

We do not run our own servers and do not store your data. Data processed by Google for advertising is handled under Google's privacy policy. Uninstalling the app removes all game data stored on your device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
